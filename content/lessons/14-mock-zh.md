# Mock ZH

This is a replica of the **sample ZH** (`lab-11/MZH.pdf`): **100 minutes**, **70 practical points** (auto-graded) and **30 theory points** (self-assessed against a model answer).

How it works:

1. Press **Start exam**. The timer starts, and hints, expected outputs and solutions are hidden.
2. Solve the tasks in any order. **Check** tells you whether a task is correct (as in a real exam, you can still fix it).
   A correct practical task is worth its full points.
3. For theory questions, write your answer in the box, then rate yourself after revealing the model answer
   (**full / half / zero** points) once you press **Finish**.
4. **Finish exam** shows your score and grade, using the course's thresholds:

| % | Grade |
|---|---|
| 0–49 | 1 (elégtelen) |
| 50–62 | 2 (elégséges) |
| 63–74 | 3 (közepes) |
| 75–86 | 4 (jó) |
| 87–100 | 5 (jeles) |

> Tip: do the whole course once before trying this. Your previous exam results are saved, so you can track your progress.
